## Pinned comment

> Two things I was careful about while making this:
>
> 1. Zimbabwe is often called "the breadbasket of Africa". Africa Check has looked
> at that and the evidence is thinner than the phrase suggests — so it's on screen
> as DISPUTED rather than stated as fact.
> 2. Land reform is genuinely contested. The land was taken under colonial rule and
> the argument for returning it was real. What this video argues is narrower: the
> way it was carried out destroyed the export earnings that paid the country's bills.
>
> Sources are in the description. Corrections welcome — I'll pin them.
