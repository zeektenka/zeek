## Deliberate editorial choices

- The script does **not** claim Zimbabwe was definitively "the breadbasket of Africa".
  It says people called it that, then immediately qualifies it — and the animation
  stamps DISPUTED over the phrase. This is the single most common error in videos
  on this topic.
- Land reform is presented with the colonial-dispossession argument stated plainly
  before the economic consequences. The two-sided card is in the edit for that reason.
- No cost figure is given for the DRC war because the credible range is wide.
- "Roughly every day" is used instead of "every 24.7 hours" — the precise figure is
  one economist's estimate, not a measurement.
