## Deliberate editorial choices

- The script says the dollar's reserve share decline is **slow** and that the dollar
  has **no serious rival yet**. The IMF's own blog notes that once adjusted for
  exchange-rate moves the decline is smaller than headline figures suggest, so
  overstating de-dollarisation would be wrong.
- "Printing money" is defined precisely (central bank reserves buying bonds) before
  any comparison is drawn, because the loose version of this comparison is the single
  most common piece of misinformation on this topic.
- The video explicitly concedes the cost of US money creation (2022 inflation) rather
  than presenting the dollar's position as free. That honesty is also what protects
  the channel from the "low-effort AI explainer" pattern-match.
