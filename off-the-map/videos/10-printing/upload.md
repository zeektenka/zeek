## Pinned comment

> A few things I was careful to get right:
>
> • "Printing money" in a rich country means a central bank creating reserves and
> buying bonds, not literally printing notes. The comparison only makes sense once
> that's clear.
> • The dollar's reserve share has fallen from above 70% at the start of the
> century to about 57% — but the IMF notes that once you adjust for exchange-rate
> moves, that decline is smaller than the headline numbers suggest. I've tried not
> to oversell de-dollarisation.
> • This video concedes the real cost: 9.1% US inflation in June 2022. Printing
> isn't free for anyone.
>
